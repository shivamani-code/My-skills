## Standing rules (do not delete)
1. The film is the site: every section is a shot, not a card grid.
2. One idea per screen. If a screen needs a paragraph, it is the wrong screen.
3. One continuous camera per scrubbed clip; cuts happen with FX (flash/burn), not hard jumps.
4. Mobile first: design at 412x915, then widen.
5. Original assets only: no film/IP characters, no copied code, no watermarks.
6. First frame visible in < 1.5 s on 4G; total first load < 3 MB.
7. Reduced motion gets a beautiful static version, not a broken one.
8. End on one clear action (contact, buy, play). Nothing after it.
